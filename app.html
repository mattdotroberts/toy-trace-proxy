Method, limits and how to extend this

What this is. A research companion for people who want their children's playthings out of supply chains linked to the occupation of Palestine. The database was compiled by hand in October 2026 from public reporting, boycott coalitions and company statements. Each entry shows its sources so you can check the work.

What the verdicts mean. Boycott call means a recognised campaign or coalition explicitly calls for boycotting the company. Documented ties means dated, sourced facts exist but no formal boycott call. Nothing documented found means exactly that: absence of evidence, not innocence. Weak or single-source items are labelled as such; we would rather show you a doubt than hide it.

What this is not. Not legal advice, not a verdict on individuals' beliefs, and not exhaustive. Personal philanthropy is noted as personal, separate from corporate conduct, and Jewishness or Israeli origin is never treated as a tie: only actions, money and statements are recorded.

Extending it. For a toy not covered, check the BDS movement's official lists at bdsmovement.net, Who Profits, and the boycott-israel.org list.
